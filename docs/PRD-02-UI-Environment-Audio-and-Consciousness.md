# Product Requirements Document

# Project Quatro — UI, Environment, Audio & Consciousness

> **Document Purpose:** Mendefinisikan identitas emosional dan sensory experience Project Quatro melalui UI/UX, environment, lighting, color, animation, soundscape, dan prinsip consciousness-driven design.
>
> **Primary Ownership:** UI / UX, Environment Direction, Audio Direction, Atmospheric Experience.
>
> **Core Principle:** Game harus terasa seperti sebuah ruang yang memberi pemain kesempatan untuk bernapas, bukan sebuah sistem yang terus meminta perhatian.

---

# 1. Design Identity

## 1.1 The Soul of Project Quatro

Project Quatro bukan hanya game tentang mobil, puzzle, dan dunia voxel.

Experience yang ingin dibangun adalah:

> **A small, warm world where something strange is happening — and the player is allowed to simply be there.**

Pemain tidak selalu harus melakukan sesuatu.

Kadang mereka hanya:

* duduk di dalam mobil,
* melihat hujan,
* mendengar ambience,
* berjalan di lorong,
* memperhatikan cahaya,
* melihat dunia berubah,
* atau berhenti sejenak.

**Stillness adalah bagian dari gameplay experience.**

---

# 2. Design Philosophy — "The Semicolon"

Semicolon (`;`) digunakan sebagai filosofi utama desain.

Dalam bahasa:

> `;` bukan akhir.
> Ia adalah jeda sebelum sesuatu berlanjut.

Dalam game:

> Pemain tidak dipaksa terus bergerak.
> Ada ruang untuk berhenti, melihat, mendengar, dan kemudian melanjutkan.

Semicolon bukan sekadar logo atau decoration.

Ia menjadi prinsip desain:

```text
ACTION
  ↓
PAUSE
  ↓
NOTICE
  ↓
FEEL
  ↓
CONTINUE
```

Bukan:

```text
ACTION → ACTION → ACTION → ACTION
```

---

# 3. Emotional Target

Setiap scene harus dievaluasi berdasarkan pertanyaan:

> "Apa yang pemain rasakan ketika berada di sini selama 30 detik tanpa melakukan apa pun?"

Jika jawabannya hanya:

* kosong,
* membosankan,
* ingin cepat pergi,

maka environment belum berhasil.

Targetnya adalah:

* nyaman,
* curious,
* warm,
* peaceful,
* subtly mysterious,
* alive.

---

# 5. Visual Principle — Warm Digital World

Voxel tidak boleh terasa seperti kumpulan kubus generik.

Voxel world harus memiliki karakter:

* soft,
* tactile,
* slightly imperfect,
* warm,
* handcrafted.

Target visual:

> **digital world with physical soul**

Bukan:

> sterile procedural voxel world.

---

# 6. Material Language

Prioritaskan material yang terasa familiar dan tactile.

## Primary Materials

* painted wood,
* warm stone,
* aged metal,
* paper,
* ceramic,
* cloth,
* glass,
* soft vegetation.

## Avoid

* overly glossy surfaces,
* sterile metallic environments,
* excessive neon,
* hyper-realistic textures,
* uniform procedural surfaces.

# 7. Color System

Color harus dibangun berdasarkan emotional temperature.

## Normal World

Palette:

* warm cream,
* muted green,
* dusty blue,
* warm gray,
* soft brown,
* muted yellow.

Tujuannya:

**familiarity.**

---

## Voxel World

Palette sedikit lebih magical tetapi tetap grounded.

Gunakan:

* soft blue,
* muted cyan,
* warm amber,
* moss green,
* lavender-gray,
* deep navy,
* warm cream.

Saturation harus dikontrol.

Jangan membuat semua objek colorful sekaligus.

---

# 8. Contrast Philosophy

Contrast digunakan untuk hierarchy.

Bukan untuk membuat semuanya terlihat "cool".

Contoh:

```text
BACKGROUND
low contrast
      ↓
ENVIRONMENT
medium contrast
      ↓
INTERACTIVE OBJECT
higher contrast
      ↓
IMPORTANT DISCOVERY
highest visual emphasis
```

Pemain harus dapat memahami:

> "Apa yang penting?"

tanpa UI besar yang berteriak.

---

# 9. Lighting

Lighting menjadi salah satu storytelling tools utama.

## Day 1

Natural morning / daylight.

Terasa normal.

## Day 2

Lighting berubah sedikit.

Contoh:

* warmer sunlight,
* longer shadows,
* unusual sky gradient.

## Day 3

Lighting mulai terasa uncanny.

Tidak harus gelap.

Justru dunia masih indah tetapi terasa "wrong".

## Voxel World

Gunakan:

* volumetric light,
* soft shadows,
* warm interior lighting,
* subtle bloom,
* ambient occlusion,
* atmospheric depth.

---

# 10. Golden Hour Principle

Golden hour dapat digunakan sebagai recurring visual motif.

Warm sunlight harus muncul pada momen tertentu:

* perjalanan pulang,
* discovery,
* reflective moments,
* major narrative transitions.

Tujuannya bukan hanya aesthetic.

Golden light menjadi visual representation dari:

> "everything is temporarily okay."

---

# 11. Environment Storytelling

Environment harus mampu menceritakan sesuatu tanpa dialog.

Contoh:

Sebuah meja memiliki:

* dua kursi,
* satu mug,
* buku terbuka,
* lampu menyala.

Pemain dapat menyimpulkan bahwa seseorang baru saja berada di sana.

Tidak perlu dialog.

---

# 12. Objects Have Presence

Objek penting harus memiliki:

* silhouette,
* material identity,
* placement,
* lighting relationship,
* subtle animation jika diperlukan.

Contoh:

### Lamp

Bukan hanya:

```text
Light Source
```

Tetapi:

```text
Lamp
+
Warm glow
+
Tiny flicker
+
Ambient hum
+
Soft shadow
```

---

# 13. Environmental Motion

Dunia harus hidup meskipun pemain diam.

Gunakan motion kecil:

* leaves moving,
* curtains moving,
* dust particles,
* clouds,
* water,
* subtle light movement,
* hanging objects,
* small NPC/environment activity.

Motion harus:

**slow + organic + irregular.**

Hindari looping yang terlalu mudah terlihat.

---

# 14. UI Philosophy

UI harus menjadi **quiet interface**.

Rule:

> UI should communicate without demanding attention.

HUD tidak boleh memenuhi layar.

---

# 15. HUD

Default HUD harus minimal.

Hanya tampilkan informasi ketika diperlukan.

Contoh:

```text
        WORLD
          
          
                [interaction]
          
          
     [minimal vehicle information]
```

Saat tidak diperlukan, elemen UI dapat fade out.

---

# 16. Interaction System

Saat pemain mendekati object:

jangan langsung menampilkan panel besar.

Gunakan:

* subtle icon,
* small prompt,
* gentle animation,
* contextual highlight.

Contoh:

```text
       ✦
    [Old Drawer]
```

bukan:

```text
╔════════════════════╗
║ PRESS E TO INTERACT║
╚════════════════════╝
```

---

# 17. Cursor / Interaction Feedback

Cursor dapat berubah berdasarkan context:

```text
DEFAULT
  ↓
LOOK
  ↓
INTERACT
  ↓
IMPORTANT
```

Animasi cursor harus subtle.

Tidak menggunakan efek arcade yang berlebihan.

---

# 18. Microinteraction Language

Setiap interaction memiliki tactile response.

### Open Drawer

* tiny movement,
* soft wood sound,
* subtle camera response.

### Pick Up Item

* object lifts slightly,
* small easing animation,
* soft sound.

### Discover Secret

* pause,
* tiny visual emphasis,
* musical accent.

### Solve Puzzle

Jangan gunakan:

> huge "SUCCESS!" screen.

Gunakan:

```text
mechanism moves
      ↓
door slowly opens
      ↓
sound changes
      ↓
new space revealed
```

The world itself confirms success.

---

# 19. Animation Philosophy

Gunakan animation curves yang:

* smooth,
* soft,
* slightly elastic,
* natural.

Avoid:

* abrupt UI movement,
* excessive bounce,
* aggressive transitions.

Everything should feel like it has **weight**.

---

# 20. Camera

Camera harus memberikan rasa:

> "Aku sedang berada di dunia ini."

Bukan:

> "Aku sedang mengontrol character model."

Driving camera:

* slightly weighted,
* smooth follow,
* subtle body movement,
* controlled FOV changes.

Walking camera:

* stable,
* low motion sickness,
* subtle movement.

Avoid excessive camera shake.

---

# 21. Driving Presentation

Quatro harus terasa seperti kendaraan yang benar-benar memiliki massa.

Visual feedback:

* body movement,
* suspension,
* wheel rotation,
* slight camera inertia,
* environment passing speed,
* subtle reflections.

Saat berhenti:

> dunia tidak berhenti.

Ambience tetap berjalan.

---

# 22. Interior / Castle Design

Castle bukan dungeon horror.

Castle adalah:

> **a mysterious old place that still feels lived in.**

Gunakan:

* warm lamps,
* wooden furniture,
* books,
* plants,
* kitchen objects,
* old paintings,
* tools,
* rugs,
* windows,
* fireplaces.

Mystery muncul dari:

* impossible architecture,
* strange symbols,
* rooms that shouldn't connect,
* voxel anomalies,
* objects behaving strangely.

# 23. Cozy Mystery Rule

Untuk setiap elemen mysterious, usahakan terdapat elemen comforting di dekatnya.

Contoh:


```text

+
WOODEN BOOKSHELF
```

# 24. Audio Identity

Audio bukan background decoration.

Audio menjadi salah satu cara dunia berkomunikasi dengan pemain.

Target:

> **warm, intimate, organic, slightly magical.**

---

# 25. Music Philosophy

Music tidak boleh terus-menerus mengatakan:

> "THIS IS AN IMPORTANT MOMENT."

Sebaliknya:

> "I am here with you."

Gunakan musik yang:

* melodic,
* warm,
* sparse,
* organic,
* repetitive in a comforting way.

---

# 26. Driving Soundscape

Saat berkendara:

Layers:

```text
ROAD
+
ENGINE
+
WIND
+
TIRE
+
DISTANT ENVIRONMENT
+
MUSIC
```

Engine sound harus terasa satisfying tetapi tidak agresif.

Road noise berubah berdasarkan surface.

Contoh:

```text
ASPHALT
→ smooth rolling sound

STONE
→ slightly rougher texture

WOOD
→ softer / hollow response

VOXEL SURFACE

```

# 28. Dynamic Music

# 32. Consciousness Layer

Consciousness harus muncul melalui:

# 33. The Player Is Allowed To Stop

Pemain dapat:

* berhenti di pinggir jalan,
* parkir,
* melihat langit,
* duduk,
* berjalan tanpa objective,
* mengeksplorasi kembali area lama.

Game tidak menghukum inactivity.

Tidak ada:

> "Hurry!"

kecuali narrative benar-benar membutuhkannya.

---

# 34. Anti-Overstimulation Rules

Jangan menumpuk:

```text
UI
+
music
+
particles
+
NPC
+
animation
+
objective
+
popup
```

semuanya pada waktu yang sama.

Gunakan hierarchy.

Jika environment sedang visually rich:

> UI harus lebih quiet.

Jika music sedang expressive:

> environment audio dapat diturunkan.

Jika puzzle sedang complex:

> visual noise harus dikurangi.

---

# 35. Attention Budget

Pemain memiliki attention yang terbatas.

Setiap scene harus memiliki:

### Primary Focus

Satu hal utama.

### Secondary Focus

Beberapa detail pendukung.

### Background

Everything else.

Contoh:

```text
PRIMARY
→ mysterious door

SECONDARY
→ books
→ lamp
→ table

BACKGROUND
→ architecture
→ particles
→ distant environment
```

---

# 36. Discoverability Without UI Spam

Interactive object harus readable melalui:

* silhouette,
* placement,
* lighting,
* animation,
* material,
* context.

Jangan mengandalkan outline neon untuk semuanya.

---

# 37. Environment as Interface

Environment dapat menggantikan UI.

Contoh:

Pemain membutuhkan objective baru.

Daripada popup:

> "Find the silver key."

Environment dapat memberikan clue:

```text

+
empty key hook
+
painting showing silver key
+
nearby drawer
```

Pemain memahami tujuan melalui dunia.

# 38. Menu Design

Menu harus terasa seperti bagian dari dunia.

Possible visual metaphor:

* notebook,
* desk,
* old computer,
* pocket device,
* map,
* journal.

```text
PLAY
SETTINGS
EXIT
```

floating over empty background jika tidak diperlukan.

# 39. Pause Experience

Pause bukan sekadar freeze.

Ketika pemain pause:

* environment dapat tetap memiliki subtle ambience,
* music dapat turun,
* background dapat blur / dim,
* UI muncul dengan tenang.

Pause harus terasa seperti:

> mengambil napas.

---

# 40. Typography

Typography harus:

* readable,
* warm,
* understated,
* distinctive.

Hierarchy:

```text
TITLE
↓
SECTION
↓
BODY
↓
MICRO LABEL
```

Avoid excessive all-caps UI.

Avoid overly futuristic gaming fonts.

---

# 41. UI Sound

UI sound harus memiliki physical metaphor.

Examples:

### Button

soft click.

### Open menu

small paper / wooden movement.

### Select

tiny tonal confirmation.

### Back

soft descending tone.

### Error

low, gentle sound.

Tidak menggunakan harsh digital beeps.

---

# 42. Accessibility

UI harus mendukung:

* readable text,
* sufficient contrast,
* scalable typography,
* reduced motion,
* audio volume controls,
* music/SFX separation,
* subtitles for important dialogue,
* clear interaction states.

Reduced-motion mode harus mengurangi:

* camera sway,
* excessive transitions,
* particles,
* screen effects.

---

# 43. Responsive Web Experience

Karena game berjalan di web, interface harus mempertimbangkan:

* desktop,
* laptop,
* different aspect ratios,
* window resizing.

Gameplay utama tetap menjadi prioritas.

UI tidak boleh bergantung pada fixed resolution.

Sebelum sebuah UI component dibuat, tanyakan:

3. Apakah UI mengganggu immersion?
4. Apakah hierarchy-nya jelas?
5. Apakah interaction terasa tactile?

Setiap area harus memiliki:

* ambience,

* music relationship,

# 49. The "30 Second Test"

Setiap scene harus diuji dengan cara berikut:

1. Masuk ke scene.
2. Jangan melakukan apa pun selama 30 detik.
3. Dengarkan.
4. Lihat lighting.
5. Perhatikan motion.
6. Perhatikan composition.

Pertanyaan:

> "Apakah tempat ini terasa seperti tempat yang benar-benar ingin ditinggali sebentar?"

Jika iya, environment berhasil.

---

# 50. The "Remove 20%" Rule

Jika sebuah scene terasa terlalu ramai:

**hapus 20% elemen.**

Jika UI terasa terlalu ramai:

**hapus 20% component.**

Jika audio terasa terlalu ramai:

**hapus satu layer.**

Jika visual terasa terlalu colorful:

**kurangi saturation sebelum menambah detail.**

Minimalism bukan kekurangan content.

Minimalism adalah **intentional selection**.

---

# 51. Core Emotional Formula

Project Quatro harus mempertahankan formula:

```text
WARMTH
+
CURIOSITY
+
STILLNESS
+
DISCOVERY
+
SLIGHT UNCANNINESS
=
PROJECT QUATRO
```

Jika salah satu terlalu dominan:

```text
Too much warmth
→ becomes generic cozy game

Too much mystery
→ becomes horror

Too much stillness
→ becomes empty

Too much discovery
→ becomes checklist game

Too much UI
→ breaks immersion

Too much visual spectacle
→ destroys intimacy
```

---

# 52. Final Design Statement

> **Project Quatro should feel less like a game that constantly asks the player to do something, and more like a small world that quietly invites the player to notice something.**

The player drives.

Then stops.

Looks around.

Hears something.

Finds something strange.

Gets curious.

Continues.

And somewhere between those moments, the player is not merely progressing through the game.

They are **present inside it.**

# 54. Definition of Done

The visual/audio/UX layer is considered successful when:

* UI feels quiet rather than intrusive.
* Environment feels lived-in.
* Voxel world feels warm rather than sterile.
* Driving feels physically satisfying.
* Audio creates a sense of place.
* Mystery exists without becoming horror.
* Players can stop without being punished.
* Important discoveries feel meaningful without excessive UI.
* The game maintains a consistent emotional identity.
* The experience feels recognizably **Project Quatro**, rather than a generic voxel game.

---

# Final Principle

> **Do not design every moment to be interesting.**
>
> **Design the world so that the player becomes interested in noticing moments.**

The game does not need to constantly say:

**"Look at this."**

It should create a world where the player naturally thinks:

**"Wait... what was that?"**

And then chooses to look.
